01. Who is responsible

The publisher of Lighthouse Beyond Idle is Alexey Sergeevich Zenkin (Зенкин Алексей Сергеевич), self-employed, Russia. LutikStudio is the studio name, not a separate legal entity. Support and privacy enquiries: al.mandarinne@mail.ru.

The publisher determines the purposes and handling of support enquiries received. Advertising and email providers participate under their own terms. This draft describes the prepared release profile; previously installed test builds may behave differently.

02. The game and local saves

The device stores progress, upgrades, story entries, settings, session times, offline earnings calculations, bonus timers and ad reward confirmations. The game has no account registration, chat, cloud-save synchronisation or publisher-operated gameplay analytics server.

A separate local record contains the versions of the rules, privacy policy and advertising notice, the declaration of being at least 16, advertising consent status, and the time and language of each decision. Your exact date of birth is not requested. A 16+ declaration is not age verification or a declaration of adulthood.

A previously saved age group, conflicting information and an old region field may remain locally. The game code does not currently request or determine your region; language is not treated as your country of residence. The age group is used inside the app by the advertising bridge to control eligibility, not sent by the game code as an ad-targeting parameter.

The game code does not automatically upload save files or local diagnostic reports to the publisher. The advertising bridge network diagnostic log is disabled in the release profile. This does not establish an absence of data processing by third-party SDKs.

03. Yandex, VK and advertising data

The prepared game integrates Yandex Mobile Ads SDK 8.5.0 and VK/myTarget through Yandex mediation (adapter 5.51.2.1, myTarget SDK 5.51.2). Dependencies include AppMetrica and MyTracker 3.6.0. The presence of a library alone does not prove that every possible field is transmitted.

After separate advertising consent and entry into the game, an activated advertising profile may preload an ad. Viewing starts only when you tap a bonus offer. The game shows the reward and its conditions; the reward is granted once after SDK confirmation. Core progress does not require watching videos.

SDKs contact advertising services directly from the device. A connection exposes the IP address and technical request information to the recipient. The advertising notice covers device and app information, technical identifiers, ad requests and display results. Purposes include loading, displaying and recording ads, measuring their effectiveness and confirming rewards. The full data set for this integration is not yet established; the data is not described as anonymous.

The release configuration disables automatic SDK initialisation, the Android Advertising ID permission, location permissions and collection, VK environment collection and positive consent signals for personalisation. Startup confirmation does not change these settings. There is no separate personalisation switch. These restrictions do not establish the absence of all technical identifiers or network traffic; an IP address may allow approximate location to be inferred.

Known advertising participants are YANDEX LLC and VK LLC through mediation. The publisher has confirmed the completion of agreements with the Yandex Advertising Network and VK. Provider terms do not replace information about actual game data or establish a single role for every operation.

Provider information: Yandex Mobile Ads SDK data, the Yandex privacy policy, the VK partner offer and VK partner terms. The advertising-account privacy policy is not treated as a complete description of player data processing.

Information still to be clarified: the complete SDK data set under our settings, recipient roles and chain, retention periods or criteria, processing countries and the applicable VK channel for player data requests. These details are not presented as verified in this draft. The publisher has deferred this clarification until after release.

04. Your choices and withdrawal

Game entry is for ages 16 and up. On the single “Age and game rules” screen, the rules and advertising consent are confirmed through separate actions. Nothing is preselected. “Enter game” checks saved decisions rather than accepting them for you. Installation, reading documents and choosing a language do not give consent.

The current model requires both confirmations for entry. You cannot enter without advertising consent. Watching each ad remains a separate choice. Before eligibility is established, the game code and Android bridge block SDK initialisation, loading and display.

To withdraw, open Menu → Privacy → About ads and select “Withdraw consent” at the end of the text. The game blocks new ad requests and displays, saves progress and withdrawal, and closes. If saving fails, a message asks you to retry; ads remain disabled.

After withdrawal, the next launch shows “Age and game rules” again. New explicit advertising consent is required to enter. Progress and earned rewards remain. Acceptance of the rules remains if their version has not changed. With a valid saved confirmation, an ordinary restart does not require a new tap; changed document versions or an unreliable record may require confirmation again.

Withdrawal does not retroactively erase data already sent to providers. An in-flight transfer may finish. A separate request may be needed to delete data held by a provider.

Review status: legal review of mandatory advertising consent for entry and processing data of players aged 16–17 is incomplete. The 16+ threshold is not an official store content rating and does not replace any legally required involvement of a representative. The app does not yet implement a separate representative-consent mechanism.

05. Enquiries and support

Use al.mandarinne@mail.ru for support and privacy enquiries. The publisher receives your email address, sender name if present, message, email metadata and any materials you voluntarily attach. These are used to reply, investigate problems and handle data requests. Advertising consent is not the basis for processing support enquiries.

Mail (VK LLC) hosts the mailbox; your own email provider also participates in delivery. Only the publisher has access to the support mailbox, and forwarding to other services is not configured. See the Mail privacy policy and general services privacy policy. Using VK for both email and advertising does not merge those purposes.

Ordinary correspondence and diagnostic attachments are deleted from the publisher’s working mailbox 3 months after the issue is resolved. Earlier deletion applies where required by law. Necessary information may be kept longer for a specific dispute or a legal requirement. This is the publisher’s retention period; it does not determine deletion from the email or advertising provider’s own systems.

Do not send passwords, keys or unnecessary personal information. An ordinary support request does not require a passport copy. Check the contents of any report you choose to send.

06. This website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. Its code contains no external analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, data-submission forms, external fonts or optional cookies. RU/EN switching uses ordinary links and stores nothing in browser storage. Reading does not require JavaScript or signing in.

GitHub, as the hosting provider, receives technical connection information when a page is opened. GitHub states that visitors' IP addresses are logged for Pages security, whether or not visitors sign in. See GitHub Pages and the GitHub privacy statement. The website therefore does not claim that no data is processed.

Links to external documents and email are followed only on your action. Those services have their own rules. Reading this website does not provide consent to in-game advertising.

07. Retention, deletion and rights

Local data stays on the device until app data is cleared or the game is uninstalled. These actions may remove progress and local copies. There is no publisher-operated cloud recovery. Withdrawing advertising consent does not itself delete progress.

For access, correction, deletion or withdrawal, email al.mandarinne@mail.ru. State whether the request concerns the game, support or website; for correspondence, include its date and subject. Before personal information is disclosed, confirmation that the request concerns you, or that you are authorised to represent someone, may be needed. If a formal request is required, the publisher will explain the procedure.

Requests are handled within applicable statutory time limits. The right to contact a supervisory authority or court remains. Uninstalling the game does not erase data previously sent to advertising, email or hosting services; their procedures apply to their processing.

Full advertising data retention and recipient details still require clarification as described in section 3. No claim is made that all data is processed in a single country.

08. Changes and document status

The Russian and English editions describe the same prepared game profile. New purposes, providers and material changes must be reflected in the documents before they apply; a new user decision is requested where required.

This edition remains a draft for review. Publishing it does not establish completion of legal review or activation of production advertising. An effective date and current document version will be stated after approval. Game terms are available separately.
