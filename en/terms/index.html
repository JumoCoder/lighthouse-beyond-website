Game terms

Revision for review: 18 October 2026

Draft documents for the upcoming release
Updated for entry from age 16 and the Yandex and VK integration. Production advertising remains disabled in the prepared profile. SDK disclosures and legal review are incomplete; an effective date for an approved edition has not been set. Test APKs are not distributed here.

1. Game and publisher

Lighthouse Beyond Idle is a single-player game about developing a lighthouse and exploring the sea. Publisher: Alexey Sergeevich Zenkin (Зенкин Алексей Сергеевич), self-employed, Russia. LutikStudio is the studio name, not a separate legal entity. Support: al.mandarinne@mail.ru.

Proposed terms: a non-exclusive right to install and run a lawfully obtained copy on personal compatible devices for non-commercial use. Ownership of the game’s intellectual property is not transferred. These terms do not grant rights to third-party advertising materials or restrict rights that cannot legally be limited by agreement.

2. Entry and age

The game is for players aged 16 and up. On one startup screen, the rules and 16+ declaration are accepted separately from advertising consent. Both decisions are required to enter. “Enter game” only checks them. Reading documents does not give consent. Data processing is described in the privacy policy.

A 16+ declaration does not establish adulthood, constitute an official store content rating or replace any required involvement of a legal representative. Legal review of mandatory consent and processing data of players aged 16–17 is incomplete. No separate representative-consent mechanism is implemented yet.

3. Progress and saves

Progress is stored locally. There are no accounts or cloud-save synchronisation. Clearing app data or uninstalling may result in lost saves; recovery from a publisher-operated server is not provided.

Light, Echo and other game values are used only inside the game. Exchange for real money or cash withdrawal is not implemented. Real-money purchases are not connected in the prepared profile; terms for future purchases must be published before they become available.

4. Rewarded advertising

Yandex and VK/myTarget are integrated through Yandex mediation. After advertising consent and entry, an activated profile may preload an ad. Display starts only after a separate tap on an in-game offer. Core progress does not require watching videos. Production requests are currently disabled in the prepared profile.

The game states the reward and its conditions. An additional reward is granted once after SDK confirmation. An error or closing without confirmation does not earn a reward or remove previously earned progress. Ad availability depends on the network and provider and is not guaranteed.

5. Withdrawing consent

Open Menu → Privacy → About ads and select “Withdraw consent”. Once progress and withdrawal are saved successfully, the game closes. The next launch shows the startup screen; new advertising consent is required to enter. Previous acceptance of the rules remains if their version has not changed.

If saving fails, the game asks you to retry and keeps ads disabled. Withdrawal does not remove progress or rewards. The privacy policy describes the consequences for data already transmitted.

6. Changes and enquiries

This is a draft for the upcoming release, not an agreement already in effect. An effective date will be stated after approval. Updates may fix errors and change game content. Material changes to the terms are communicated before they apply and require a new confirmation where necessary.

Indefinite support for every Android version and continuous availability of third-party ads are not promised. The publisher is responsible within applicable law; these terms do not exclude liability that cannot legally be excluded. Questions and complaints may be sent to support. This does not restrict the right to contact a competent court or public authority.
